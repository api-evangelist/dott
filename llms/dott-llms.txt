# Dott

> Dott is a European shared micromobility operator running electric scooters and e-bikes across 250+ cities in Europe and the Middle East (formed from the 2024 Dott + TIER merger). It publishes real-time fleet data through the open GBFS 2.3 standard.

Dott's developer surface is the GBFS 2.3 feed set at https://gbfs.api.ridedott.com/public/v2. Public feeds are open (rotating vehicle IDs); an authenticated partner variant serves stable IDs. All feeds are read-only HTTP GET returning the GBFS envelope `{ last_updated, ttl, version, data }`.

## APIs
- [Dott GBFS API](https://ridedott.dev): real-time shared-mobility feeds (vehicles, types, pricing, zones, stations)
